The core of asset liability management in about 30 minutes, on balance sheets built from real 10-Ks

A training game: run a real US life insurer through five quarters of stress, ask an AI team, sign the trades, explain the result.

How the agents work

Vera sends each question to the one or two right specialists; the engine owns every number

Seven roles, one model (gpt-6-luna). Specialists read only their side of the balance sheet through read-only tools. Swap Dogg can draft a ticket; only the learner signs. A separate examiner marks explanations; Seamore reads 10-Ks with code-checked quotes. Team 19/19 facts vs Solo 16/19, at 3.5x the cost: kept for teaching, so learners weigh biased experts.
